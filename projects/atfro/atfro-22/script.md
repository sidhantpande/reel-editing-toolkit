**SPOKEN SCRIPT — exact dialogu**e  
0–04s | This website looks impressive. But does it help a visitor become a real business conversation?  
04–15s | First, clarity: can I tell who this business serves and what problem it solves without scrolling for a minute?  
15–28s | Second, trust: is there evidence that matches the promise—real work, a credible process or useful answers?  
28–40s | Third, the handoff: is the next step easy on mobile, and does the business have someone responsible for the enquiry?  
40–50s | Those are connected growth problems, not just design problems. DM AUDIT with your website and business category for a fit check.
