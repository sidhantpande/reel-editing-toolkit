## DAY 11 — 26 OCT | WHY THREE SHOTS CAN BE BETTER THAN THIRTY

Series / goal: Process trust for schools    |    Speaker: Editor / CMO    |    Length: 35–40s  
**SPOKEN SCRIPT — exact dialogu**e  
0–04s | Thirty clips don't automatically give a school a stronger story.  
04–13s | The real question is what a parent should understand after watching. Is it teamwork, confidence, learning or something else?  
13–27s | Once the school and our team agree on that objective, we capture a beginning, the learning in action and a clear payoff—using approved footage.  
27–34s | The editing should support what the school wants to communicate, not distract from it. And we learn from the questions the content creates.  
34–39s | That's how one school video becomes part of a longer communication plan.
