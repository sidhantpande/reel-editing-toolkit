# Dhiraj — retained editing lessons

The user's explicit corrections govern the batch. `03_reels/dhiraj/style-approval.json` records the approved caption style, the Reel01 opening **5–10**, Reel04's rejected **इसका मतलब** lead-in and Reel06's rejected first **joint** attempt. Never restore these from an old transcript, source script or previous export.

For subsequent reels, review the speech immediately before every repeated phrase and every source join. A long ASR word can span a false start, silence and the later clean word; spelling agreement between decoders does not prove clean timing. Use isolated short passes, chronological mouth/gesture frames, source waveform and the user's correction together. Retain the complete clean pickup, negations, qualifications and final syllable. Exclude recording commands such as Start, cut, ये एक sentence and कर दो. Record exact source-clock excluded ranges and frame-exact joins; preserve originals and previous versions.

Do not apply one reel's corrected number to another reel. Counts, prices, ages and ranges belong to their own recording. Curate the source word times before mapping captions through the new edit. Check first/last words at every cut; ASR can stretch a word over a pause or translate Hindi into English instead of transcribing it.

Match returned B-roll by inspected visual subject, not its filename alone. In the07–12 delivery, R011 is the gel footage for Reel10; R0102 is the travel overhead for Reel11; R012/R0122 reverse skincare/sports. Keep source-path/hash provenance and mute native B-roll sound. Do not recycle one unrelated object simply to fill a gap.

Use the approved Dhiraj premium white/gold system with restrained editorial italics and meaning-led groups. On ivory use dark ink and place full shaped glyphs in the quiet lower area. Inspect both entry and exit contrast in encoded frames. Keep captions out of faces, logos and B-roll objects. A layout bounding-box check supports but does not replace looking at the encoded groups and word motion.

Podcast crops follow the actual speaking person. Do not crossfade two faces. Preserve the child's and doctor's original voices, pitch and speed. Use conservative cleanup, quiet ducked original music and sparse B-roll cues. Do not generate a family likeness or add unrecorded clinical promises.

Before promoting a new version, run plan preflight and full encoded decode/frame/canvas/fps/A-V/black/loudness checks; inspect all caption groups, cuts, B-roll boundaries, selected consecutive animation frames and the complete closing. Run independent encoded speech checks, then isolated passes when a long decode omits or repeats text. Preserve source/PCM evidence. Never equate ASR, contact sheets or numeric loudness with human listening approval.
