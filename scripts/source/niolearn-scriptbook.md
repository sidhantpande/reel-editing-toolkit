# **3 · Reel production standards**

### **UNIVERSAL STRUCTURE**

* Hook in the first 3 seconds: a spoken line, an on-screen text line and a visual that moves. Never open with a logo.

* One idea per reel. Three to five beats. Each beat gets one on-screen text line (max 5–6 words).

* End card (2 sec): Midnight Navy, reverse logo, “Navigate More Possibilities.”, niolearn.com. The voice-over CTA plays over it.

* CTA types we rotate: Comment (a word or an answer) · Save · Share/Tag · Visit niolearn.com. Use one CTA per reel.

### **LOOK AND FEEL**

* Frame: 9:16, 1080 × 1920\. Keep text inside the safe zone (about 250 px from top and 380 px from bottom) so UI does not cover it.

* Text overlays in Sora Bold. White on Midnight Navy, or Midnight Navy on Cloud White. Highlight one keyword in Compass Teal (Horizon Aqua on dark).

* Subtitles: always on, Hinglish in Roman script, 1–2 lines, no emoji inside subtitles.

* Backdrops: clean navy or Cloud White set, soft practical lights. No clutter, no competitor logos or brochures with visible names.

* Pace: a cut or a text change every 2–3 seconds. Keep one breathing beat for puzzles.

* Audio: voice first. Instrumental bed at roughly 10–15 percent. Prefer licence-safe tracks. Trending audio only where it fits the tone (the Brand Book voice is calm and credible, not hyped).

### **HINGLISH WRITING RULES**

* Write the way a good mentor speaks to a 17-year-old and their parent: simple, warm, direct. Short sentences.

* Keep exam and admission terms in English (entrance, mock, diagnostic, interview). Keep emotion and explanation in Hindi.

* No fake urgency, no guarantees, no school bashing. If we are not sure, we say “verify on the official site”.

* Captions: one line hook, two lines of value, one line CTA, then hashtags. Base hashtags: \#NioLearn \#NavigateMorePossibilities \#ModernTechAdmissions plus topic tags.
