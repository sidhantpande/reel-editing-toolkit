# HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

This V2 scriptbook replaces the agency-centric narrative with ATFRO's growth-partner model. It connects business diagnosis, strategy, execution, technology, follow-up and review. It includes 14 rewritten spoken Reels plus LinkedIn, carousel and Story scripts. Read the dialogue naturally and tailor approved examples to the actual client context.  
Working rule: trust beats vanity. Use verified evidence, clear ownership, real footage and one specific CTA. Do not show any private client records, student/patient identities or sensitive business dashboards without permission. Existing GTKA and Studio footage is assumed previously posted; this is new content, not duplicate reuploads.  
All speaking timings are estimates. Read aloud at a natural pace; shorten lines rather than speed-reading. English-forward copy is deliberate for school owners, doctors and other B2B clients; presenters may naturally code-switch to Hinglish without changing the meaning.

# THE ATFRO GROWTH-PARTNER POSITIONING FRAMEWORK

# Positioning statement: ATFRO is a growth partner for businesses. We work alongside business owners and their teams to identify what is holding progress back, then design and execute the right mix of strategy, communication, digital systems and measurement within an agreed scope.

# What we are NOT saying: ATFRO does not claim ownership of every client business function, guarantee leads or revenue, or present content volume as the ultimate business outcome. Shared responsibility, commercial fit and honest reporting matter.

# Partnership journey: DISCOVER the actual problem → ALIGN on the goal and roles → BUILD the right response → LAUNCH the work → LEARN from relevant signals → IMPROVE with the client.

# Core editorial rule: Every post should answer at least three questions. Which business challenge does this address? What does ATFRO actually contribute? What next step can the audience take without being oversold?

# Story angle: Don't repeat 'we are a growth partner' in every video. Demonstrate the difference through joint planning, operational decisions, working technology, client-approved proof and improvements made after feedback.

# Primary audiences: (1) school owners and leadership teams, (2) doctors and clinic owners, (3) SME founders and operators; adapt each example to the real challenges of that audience.

# Verification: No claim of admissions, patient outcomes, ROI, lead multiplication or working beta features may be published without evidence and necessary consent. Any \[BRACKETED TEXT\] is a production placeholder and must be filled or removed before posting.

## DAY 19 — 3 NOV | LINKEDIN — HEALTHCARE PROCESS NOTE

WHAT A TRUSTWORTHY CLINIC CONTENT PROCESS REQUIRES  
**POST COP**Y  
The standard for healthcare content must be different from the standard for a typical trending Reel.

A confusing sentence can misinform someone about their care. An overconfident claim can damage trust. An identifiable patient image can create a privacy problem.

Our preferred workflow for clinic content is deliberately slower at the right stages:  
• Start with a question a patient is likely to ask.  
• Have a qualified clinician review the explanation.  
• Script for clarity, not medical sensationalism.  
• Record approved footage with informed consent where relevant.  
• Review the edit before publication.  
• Measure communication outcomes separately from clinical outcomes.

Good healthcare communication helps people understand what to ask their doctor; it shouldn't act like personal medical advice.

# PART IV — REEL RECORDING AND EDITING STANDARD

### FRAME AND DELIVERY

Record 9:16 vertical, ideally 4K when available, deliver 1080×1920. Leave room at the top for a 3–5 word hook and keep essential captions away from the bottom interface area. Shoot a clean 10-second room-tone sample on each recording day.  
Use real offices, real team members, working product screens and consented client assets. Do not generate a realistic-looking fake client case study or show fabricated numerical proof.  
Prefer natural light or a soft key light, neutral brand tones, consistent skin color, lavalier audio and restrained background music. Avoid over-designed glitch effects.  
Do one full A-roll take, then two alternate hook takes, then hands / work / side-angle B-roll. For every script, capture: 1 wide interview, 1 medium speaking angle, 3 contextual work details and 2 clean cutaways.

### SUBTITLE RULE

Burn in accurate, sentence-level subtitles; highlight only a key word when it helps comprehension. Two lines maximum where possible. Use the verified ATFRO brandbook for final typeface and colors.

### REEL COVER RULE

One strong hook, 3–6 words. Never put a dense paragraph on the cover. On Instagram, choose a portrait-safe title area and check the grid crop before publishing.

### CAPTION RULE

Start with a plain-language pain point; give one useful insight; end with one deliberate action. Avoid a wall of hashtags or unprovable guarantee.

### EXPORT RULE

Save a watermark-free master, then export native versions for Instagram and YouTube Shorts. Publish a tighter professional edit to LinkedIn only where appropriate; use subtitles and a separate context-led post. Do not carry the Instagram interface or Shorts watermark across platforms.
